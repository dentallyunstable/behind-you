# Behind You

A one-handed, first-person endless runner. You're running through a dark forest at night with only a flashlight.
Something is chasing you. You will never see what it is.

## How to play

**Level 1: The Forest.** Your truck is parked by the highway, 2000 meters away. Make it there.
The last few hundred meters are the hardest. When you see the orange streetlight through the trees, you're close.
After you escape once, **Endless** unlocks (on the escape screen, and on The Forest's card under LEVELS):
the truck won't start, and you run on into the forest past the road, as far as you can.

You start sitting by your campfire. Tap (or press any key) to look around: something is out there in the dark. Then you run.
The gear button on the start screen shows the controls. After you're caught, RUN AGAIN puts you straight back on your feet,
MAIN MENU takes you back to the fire.

Open `index.html` in any browser (phone or computer). No install is needed.

- **Phone:** put your thumb anywhere on the screen and drag (the joystick).
  - left / right: steer
  - push up: jump
  - pull down: slow down
  - hold the **FASTER** button (bottom right) with your other thumb to sprint. The ring around it is your stamina.
    Sprint it nearly empty and you're out of breath: no sprinting, and slower than your normal run until half of it is back.
- **Computer:** arrow keys or WASD, Space or up to jump, Shift to sprint.

Hitting a tree makes you stumble and lets it get closer. Listen to your heartbeat and breathing.
When the screen edges turn red, the view narrows and the flashlight starts flickering, it's right behind you.
Headphones are recommended.

Every so often you can't help looking back. Sometimes there's nothing there. Sometimes there is.
Looking back costs you: it gains on you while you look.

Fallen trees lie across the path: jump over them, or trip and lose ground.
Now and then you pass places somebody left behind: a cabin, a rusted car, a broken fence, an empty tent.

There is always an open way through the trees, even when the forest gets thick.

## Files

- `index.html`: the page
- `css/style.css`: menus, HUD, joystick
- `js/textures.js`: small low-res textures generated by code
- `js/audio.js`: sounds (generated by code, plus the breathing recording in `js/breathing-data.js`)
- `js/geo.js`: small helpers for building low-poly models
- `js/props.js`: fallen logs and the abandoned places (cabin, car, fence, tent)
- `js/world.js`: the forest (trees, logs, places, the always-open path, collisions)
- `js/camp.js`: the campfire you start at
- `js/finish.js`: the end of the level: the highway, the streetlight, your truck
- `js/art.js`: pixel art for the menus (the chained, padlocked levels)
- `js/monster.js`: the thing that follows you
- `js/game.js`: the player, flashlight, the chaser, controls and main loop
- `lib/three.min.js`: the [three.js](https://threejs.org) 3D library (r128, MIT license)

## Putting it online

To play on your phone, turn on **GitHub Pages** for this repository (Settings → Pages → deploy from this branch).
You'll get a link you can open on any phone.
