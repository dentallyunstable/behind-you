CONTROLS

PHONE

Drag your thumb anywhere: the joystick

← → steer
↑ push up to jump
↓ pull down to slow down

Hold FASTER (bottom right) with your other thumb to sprint

COMPUTER

← → or A D: steer
↑ W or Space: jump
↓ or S: slow down
Shift: sprint

STAMINA

The ring around FASTER is your stamina. Use it all up and you'll be out of breath: slower than your normal run until you catch your breath.

Headphones recommended
